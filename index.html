What we quote

Five categories from multiple manufacturers. Origin, warranty and safety documentation come from the manufacturer and are stated per product.

  • Playground systems

    Structures, freestanding equipment, components

  • Shade structures

    Hip, sail, cantilever, umbrellas, replacement canopies

  • Shelters

    Park and picnic shelters, stamped for wind and snow load

  • Site amenities

    Benches, tables, receptacles, grills, bike shelters, dog park, fitness

  • Greenhouses

    Commercial greenhouses for school gardens, parks programs and grounds departments. Ask for current manufacturers and sizes.

ADA-accessible options are a filter, not a slogan. We flag them per product as the manufacturer documents them; the accessibility of a finished site also depends on surfacing, routes and layout.

Who's buying?

The install window is the summer break.

Districts buy on board approval and a bond or facilities budget, then need the work finished before students return. We quote playground systems and shade over play areas line by line, with the manufacturer's safety documentation per item, so facilities can take a single packet to the board.

Usually quoted: playground structures, shade over play areas, benches and tables.

Quote for a district

Grant-funded? Lines you can set against the award.

Parks projects often run on a grant with its own line items and closeout date. We split the quote to match, and include origin statements where the grant asks for them. Small replacements, a canopy or a grill, can usually go on a purchase card.

Usually quoted: picnic shelters, park grills, shade sails, greenhouses.

Quote for a parks project

FAR and DFARS clauses, origin per line item.

For installations, federal campuses and visitor areas. We answer solicitations within posted deadlines, quote against the clauses in the package and supply country-of-origin documentation per line. IUID marking is available when the contract requires it.

Usually quoted: site furnishings, shelters, shade structures.

Common areas built for daily use.
